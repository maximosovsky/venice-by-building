# Church route follow-up evidence review

## Verified decisions implemented

- **Cristo Re, Castello:** Italian `oratorio di Cristo Re` and English `Cristo Re, Venice` reconcile as one church, `mention-cristo-re-celestia`. Cached OSM way **582322560** explicitly names *Chiesa di Cristo Re alla Celestia*, building=church, at 45.4380991,12.3493237. GCatholic's cached diocesan list independently identifies Cristo Re alla Celestia (church 47596). Do not adopt its OSM Q385975 tag without independent entity verification. Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiese_di_Venezia ; https://gcatholic.org/churches/italia/47596 ; https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/582322560 .
- **Santa Maria del Soccorso, Dorsoduro:** Italian and English mentions reconcile as one surviving adapted church, `mention-soccorso-dorsoduro`. La Biennale describes the completely renovated seventeenth-century church and independent ground-floor entrance at Fondamenta del Soccorso. Cached GCatholic 63813 and its diocesan map encode token `KgbnTM89I`; actual published `gcmap.js` decoder yields **45.433225,12.320225**. This is a building reference, not a surveyed entrance. https://labiennale.org/en/noticeboard/2196 ; https://gcatholic.org/churches/italia/63813 .
- **Santa Maria delle Grazie, Burano:** included adapted surviving church, `mention-grazie-burano`. Cached Wikipedia Burano explicitly states church/Cappuccine founded 1533, closed to worship in 1806, restored and reopened for exhibitions in 2006. Exact named OSM church way **176657496**, 45.4838241,12.4172199. https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burano ; https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/176657496 .
- **San Vitale, Poveglia:** exclude demolished church, not false absence. Italian Poveglia states *Dell'antica pieve di San Vitale resta solo il campanile*. Replace generic disambiguation Q18427208 with contextual `mention-san-vitale-poveglia` in reconciliation. https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poveglia .
- **San Cristoforo di Venezia:** exclude demolished San Cristoforo della Pace monastery/church; not San Michele in Isola. Municipal cemetery history states the existing monastery was demolished and terrain levelled for the cemetery after 1807. Search retrieval returned actual municipal passage; direct HTTP returned Incapsula instead and is not the evidence. https://www.comune.venezia.it/it/content/cimitero-smichele .
- **San Teodoro Q119156487:** retain, but explicitly mark **surviving_chapel / restricted**. Cached Italian article distinguishes demolished medieval predecessor from the Renaissance chapel begun 1486 behind basilica apses, restored 1961, partly incorporated into Ducal Palace structures, accessible through the basilica canonica and San Pietro chapel. It is not only a historical footprint. https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiesa_di_San_Teodoro_(Venezia) .
- **Lido generic identities:** Q501107 is Nativity of Mary (feast), Q128267 is Saint Joseph (person), not buildings. Preserve their real contextual candidates as `mention-nascente-lido` and `mention-san-giuseppe-lido`; do not exclude them merely because the lookup selected a generic article.

## Seven unresolved identities — do not assert completeness

1. **Oratorio Santa Maria della Salute, Castello:** real oratory beside Santa Maria Formosa. Local historical source describes merging the Scuola San Giosafat and Purificazione in 1833; address reference Fondamenta dei Preti 5264. https://www.conoscerevenezia.it/?p=98950 . Beware BeWeB **2203**, which has the same name but official location fragment is **45.448806,12.199056**, mainland, NOT Castello. Raw evidence saved as `be_salute.html` and `be_salute_location.html`.
2. **Santissima Trinità, Giudecca:** surviving Clarisse church supported by https://www.veneziamuseo.it/terra/santa_croce/crose/crose_ord_clarisse.htm (raw `clarisse.html`), historical/current description and address San Giacomo 208. Browser-read independent directory returns **45.425516,12.330804**, but says address unconfirmed; this was not promoted to verified building geometry. https://chiese.milazzo.life/chiesa-monastero-della-santissima-trinita-clarisse-giudecca-venezia-ve-10000901/ links official mass source https://oldpatriarcatovenezia.glauco.it/vicariato-s-polo-s-croce-dorsoduro-orari-ss-messe/ . Q122152496 is a different mainland oratory, not this building.
3. **Santa Barbara e Santa Maria di Loreto, Sant'Andrea:** Italian Wikipedia list explicitly calls it an oratory in Sant'Erasmo parish. No exact surviving building geometry verified; do not merge with Sant'Eurosia Vignole or Santa Barbara Burano.
4. **Sacro Cuore di Gesù, Lido:** operator monastery site supports real religious community, official address **Via Navarino 14** and approach via Via Algarotti; cached `fcj_17.html`. https://www.figliedelcuoredigesuvenezia.it/?id=17&lang=it . Church geometry not verified. Homepage's lengthy Rome chapel description must not be mistaken for Venice evidence.
5. **Santa Maria Nascente, Lido:** Wikipedia list locates at Ospedale al Mare; Lido article independently describes a church inside former hospital. Redevelopment coverage explicitly names surviving Santa Maria Nascente intended for reopening: https://campaignforalivingvenice.org/2023/04/21/plan-for-a-medical-technopark-at-the-ex-ospedale-al-mare-del-lido-made-public . Geometry not verified; generic Q501107 removed.
6. **San Giuseppe, Lido cemetery:** Wikipedia list says oratorio del cimitero. Municipal site locates San Nicolò cemetery at Via Cipro 72, but this does not locate the specific chapel. Generic Q128267 removed. https://www.comune.venezia.it/it/content/servizi-e-ispettorato-cimiteri-venezia-mestre .
7. **Opera della Carità di Santa Maria del Mare:** operator and Patriarchate identify a real care institution at Strada Comunale dei Murazzi 3. Operator page does not establish a separately locatable church; institutional existence must not be transformed into a temple coordinate or absence. https://www.osmc.org/index.php/centro-servizi-per-anziani-casa-dell-ospitalita-pellestrina ; https://www.patriarcatovenezia.it/vita-pastorale/case-per-anziani/ . Raw operator page `osmc.html`.
